The tale behind the blue orangutan

Every book I read has the same companion: a slightly battered bookmark shaped like a blue orangutan. He has held my place through doorstop novels, slim poetry pamphlets and everything in between, so when it came to naming this blog there was only ever one choice.

The North West has always been rich with poets and storytellers, and I am proud to be writing among them. Expect reviews of the books on my shelf, poems of my own and the occasional love letter to the Manchester writing scene.
