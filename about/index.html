A poet and book lover

I'm Ellie, a creative writer and poet living in Manchester. Words are both my day job and my favourite way to waste an evening.

Blue Orangutan Tales is named after the blue orangutan bookmark that lives in whatever I happen to be reading. He has seen me through hundreds of books, and now he lends his name to the place where I write about them.

The North West has a long line of poets and storytellers who write plainly and feel deeply, and that is the tradition I want to write in. On this site you will find my book reviews, my poems and occasional notes on the Manchester and wider North West writing scene.

If you would like to keep up between posts, Instagram is where everything appears first.

Follow @blueorangutantales

What you will find here

Book reviews. Honest thoughts on literary fiction, poetry collections and whatever else makes it to the top of the pile. If I loved it, I will say so. If I did not, I will say that too, kindly.

Poems. Original poetry of my own, published here as each piece is finished.

Tales. Notes from a reading and writing life in the North West: libraries, bookshops, open mic nights and the occasional rained-off festival.
