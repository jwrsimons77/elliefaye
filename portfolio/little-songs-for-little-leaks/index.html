Giggle Knickers

The Issue

In the UK, 1 in 3 women over 30 suffer from stress incontinence. Little leaks that are perfectly normal.

Every day thousands of women go to extraordinary lengths to avoid any situation that may increase their risk of experiencing a leak. Essentially stopping them from doing what they love.

The Solution

Funny songs for the radio!

To stand out against the beige wall of incontinence advertising we created a series of humorous songs that took a refreshingly honest look at what a lot of women already deal with and laughed about it along with them.

This series of giggle-packed radio ads raises awareness about the realities of sensitive bladders, encouraging women to change their knickers, not their lifestyle.
