Instructions for Haunting a City

July 2026 ✦ 2 min read ✦ Written on the 192

i.m. every flat I ever left a plant in

First, die a little. Nothing dramatic — a job that ends, a lease that doesn't renew, a Tuesday that closes over your head like canal water.
Then return. Haunting is mostly commuting. Take the bus you used to take. Sit where you sat. Let the city pretend it doesn't know you, the way an ex does, badly, across a crowded Aldi.
Touch nothing. You are here to be weather. Fog the same windows. Rain on the same queue. Stand outside the flat with the new curtains and forgive them, if you can, for watering your plant.
And when the last bar you loved becomes a luxury gym, do what ghosts do best: stay. Be the cold spot by the treadmills. Be the reason the lights flicker every time someone says the old name of the street.

— E.F., top deck, somewhere past Levenshulme
