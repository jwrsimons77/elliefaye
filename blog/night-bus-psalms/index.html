Night Bus Psalms

May 2026 ✦ 3 min read ✦ Three psalms

psalm i — for the drunk choir

Blessed are the six lads on the back seat murdering a ballad from 2009, for they have remembered every word and none of the shame.
Let the whole bus pretend annoyance. Let the whole bus mouth the chorus.

✦

psalm ii — for the window fog

Somebody has drawn a heart in the breath on the glass, and somebody else, later, an arrow through it — and this, your honour, is the case for the defence of the entire human race.

✦

psalm iii — for the driver

And blessed, quietly, is the driver who watches the mirror an extra second, holding the whole night still until you've sat down —
who will never know your name, and carried you home anyway. Amen. Ring the bell. Amen.

— E.F., the 2am service, upper deck
