Some of the best bits

End The Wait

HER2-mutated advanced NSCLC is rare and deadly cancer mutation that hasn't seen any progress in over 20 years. The market is truly stagnated, but with a lagging campaign, we finally broke through the stasis.

Sans Gender

Despite growing gender-inclusivity, our language is still dripping with unconscious bias.

To tackle this unrecognised issue, we hijacked Open Type code to create a typeface that actively removed unnecessarily gendered words as they were typed.

The Welcoming Doors Project

People living with HIV deserve to feel safe everywhere, especially in mental health settings. However, 59% have experienced stigma in these environments.

To drive education and support people living with HIV, we created Welcoming Doors. A campaign to promote the Breaking Barriers in HIV Care eLearning course.

Through this project, I collaborated with people living with HIV and a diverse range of illustrators to bring safe spaces that accurately communicate the experiences of HIV to life.

Young Cannes Lion 2022

Placing second in the Young Cannes Lions, we beat hundreds of other up-and-comers across the country.

Our entry, Your Mark Matters, set out to prove diversity surveys aren't just another box-ticking exercise, but a box-breaking act of inclusivity.
