Open with a paragraph that names the book and the author, and says in a
sentence what you made of it. This first paragraph does double duty: it is
what readers see first and what search engines use to understand the review.

The kraft-paper details patch (author, genre, pages, rating stars) appears
automatically above this text, built from the details at the top of the file.

Write as many paragraphs as you like. **Bold** and *italics* work, and a
favourite quote can be set like this:

> "You can't please everyone. The second you change it so one person likes
> it, you'll lose someone who liked it before. You have to learn how to
> listen to yourself."

Close with who you would press the book onto, and what you are reading next.
